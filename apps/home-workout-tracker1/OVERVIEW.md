# Home Workout Tracker

A comprehensive home fitness monitoring and workout management system designed for users who want to track their workouts, progress, and health metrics from home — without needing a gym membership.

## Who it's for
- Fitness enthusiasts who work out at home
- Anyone starting a fitness journey
- People tracking body measurements, BMI, and workout progress

## What it does
- **Exercise Library**: 30+ home exercises with step-by-step instructions, categorized by muscle group, difficulty, and equipment
- **Workout Tracker**: Interactive workout session flow with set/rep logging and rest timer
- **Progress Tracking**: Charts for weight, BMI, calories, workout frequency, and duration
- **BMI Calculator**: Calculate and track BMI history over time
- **Body Measurements**: Track weight, chest, waist, arms, hips, thighs
- **Fitness Goals**: Set and track personalized fitness targets with progress bars
- **Workout Calendar**: Visual calendar showing workout history
- **Achievements & Streaks**: Gamification with badges and streak tracking
- **Workout Plans**: Create custom plans based on goal, level, and equipment
- **Reports**: Generate and download fitness reports
- **Reminders**: Set workout reminder schedules
- **Admin Dashboard**: Manage users, exercises, and view platform statistics
